**1) Valuing population‑health data as an intangible asset on public providers’ balance sheets—implications for WACC and investment under EHDS/DGA.**  
_Gap:_ VBHC depends on outcomes and longitudinal data, yet hospitals and integrated care entities in Beveridgean systems treat data creation/curation as an expense, not a capital asset, obscuring true ROI and depressing investment; accounting theory (IAS 38/IPSAS) is silent on routine recognition of internally generated datasets, while EU data policy (EHDS/DGA) materially increases their usability/option value. 
_Idea:_ build a **“shadow asset register”** for clinical and PROMs/PREMs datasets using OECD data‑asset valuation approaches (cost, income, market proxies), and model how recognizing (or disclosing) data capital changes the cost of capital and capital budgeting thresholds for VBHC projects. 
_Method & data:_ combine IFRS/IPSAS rules analysis, OECD valuation frameworks, and a panel of NHS/SNS digital programs with event‑style disclosure shocks (e.g., registry launches) to estimate effects on investment intensity and hurdle rates. 
_Contribution:_ a finance‑based mechanism that makes “investing in outcomes‑ready data” bankable. ([IFRS](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-38-intangible-assets/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "IAS 38 Intangible Assets - IFRS"))

**2) Value‑based procurement (VBP) and suppliers’ cost of capital: do outcomes‑linked contracts lower medtech WACC in Beveridgean systems?**  
_Gap:_ We know VBP can shift selection toward total value, but we lack corporate‑finance evidence on whether **outcomes‑based/whole‑life** contracts reduce suppliers’ cash‑flow volatility and financing costs; simultaneously, payment regimes (e.g., NHS Payment Scheme/API) alter providers’ risk. 
_Idea:_ estimate WACC changes for suppliers winning VBP lots versus price‑only tenders; test how API payment certainty and system‑level social‑value rules affect cash‑flow beta. 
_Method & data:_ difference‑in‑differences on UK frameworks (Supply Chain & ICS), linking award data to suppliers’ spreads/equity betas; structural cash‑flow models with performance‑payment clauses. 
_Contribution:_ shows how contract design transmits to capital markets, informing VBP templates to crowd‑in private investment aligned with VBHC. ([IRP CDN](https://irp.cdn-website.com/28282eca/files/uploaded/AXREM_VBP_Paper_2025_Final.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "VBP Paper 2025 Final"))

**3) Outcomes‑based finance for prevention: redesigning Social/Health Impact Bonds with _PROMs/PREMs_ triggers.**  
_Gap:_ Evidence on social/health impact bonds is mixed and seldom uses clinically robust VBHC outcomes; most contracts trigger on process or crude endpoints, weakening attribution and investor appetite. 
_Idea:_ engineer **PROM‑anchored outcomes contracts** (e.g., frailty, severe asthma sets) with transparent risk adjustment; compare their risk/return to legacy SIBs and to budget‑holder baselines. 
_Method & data:_ compile the GO Lab global dataset; build synthetic controls; simulate payout distributions under PROMs‑based triggers; cost‑effectiveness overlay. 
_Contribution:_ a blueprint for _financeable_ prevention in Beveridgean systems that pays for **measurable value** rather than activity. **4) Real options for digital hospitals: staging EMR/AI decisions under policy and technology uncertainty.**  
_Gap:_ Digital hospital appraisals rarely incorporate **option value** (stage, defer, expand/abandon) despite volatile tech performance and regulation; VBHC needs digital rails, but poor ex‑ante valuation stalls investment. 
_Idea:_ apply compound real‑options to NHS/SNS digital programs (EHR, e‑referrals, AI triage), calibrating volatility with audit/uptake data; test whether option‑aware portfolios dominate NPV‑only portfolios in net benefit and downside protection. 
_Method & data:_ EU Court of Auditors and OECD digital‑health indicators; new CBA frameworks; portfolio simulation. 
_Contribution:_ a capital‑budgeting method tailored to VBHC digital infrastructure with policy‑relevant thresholds. ([European Court of Auditors](https://www.eca.europa.eu/ECAPublications/SR-2024-25/SR-2024-25_EN.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Special report 25/2024: Digitalisation of healthcare"))

**5) Working‑capital stress and receivables finance in Beveridgean providers: when do arrears destroy VBHC investment capacity?**  
_Gap:_ Supplier‑payment delays and arrears persist in parts of Europe, but we lack a corporate‑finance mapping from **payment discipline → working capital → capex for value‑enabling projects**; EU Late Payment rules exist yet enforcement is uneven.

**10) Financing “AI‑as‑a‑Service” for diagnostics and pathway optimization: outcome‑based subscriptions under EU MDR/UK MHRA and NICE ESF.**  
_Gap:_ Hospitals face opex commitments for AI tools with uncertain real‑world performance; regulatory frameworks (NICE ESF; MDR/MDCG; MHRA) exist, but **contracting/financing** models rarely tie payments to _measured outcomes_ or lifecycle updates—key for VBHC and for vendor valuation.
