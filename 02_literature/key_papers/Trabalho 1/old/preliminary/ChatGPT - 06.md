## 6) **Integrated providers as internal capital markets: the 2024 Portuguese ULS reform as a quasi‑natural experiment in capital allocation for VBHC**

**Motivation & gap.** 
In January 2024 Portugal generalized the _Unidades Locais de Saúde_ (ULS) model—integrating primary and hospital care into single E.P.E. entities—after a new framework law (DL 102/2023) that lists specific hospital–ACES mergers and sets the integrated‑care mandate; the SNS formally announced the nationwide rollout from **1 January 2024** and, subsequently, restructured upstream governance by extinguishing the ARS regional authorities (DL 54/2024). ACSS operational circulars document transitional changes to billing and a **centralized payment model** for ULS and IPOs, while other circulars introduced **risk stratification** and a new e‑Quality platform—both instruments consistent with VBHC measurement. Despite this re‑wiring of budgets and responsibilities, we lack causal, corporate‑finance‑oriented evidence on how _integration_ changes **internal capital markets**: the allocation of working capital and capex between levels of care, investment in “value infrastructure” (registries, PROMs/PREMs, digital rails), and exposure to payment‑flow risk. This paper treats ULS formation as a structural shock to the internal capital market of public providers and asks whether integration reallocates capital toward VBHC enablers and prevention. ([Diário da República](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/102-2023-223906278?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Decreto-Lei n.º 102/2023, de 7 de novembro | DR - Diário da República"))

**Testable hypotheses.** 
_H1:_ Integration increases the ULS share of investment directed to VBHC enablers—proxied by digital/measurement capex and PROMs/PREMs rollout—relative to pre‑reform baselines and to non‑integrated benchmarks (e.g., IPOs). 
_H2:_ Integration reduces financing frictions—proxied by investment–cash‑flow sensitivity and arrears days—via centralized payment mechanisms. 
_H3:_ Integration shifts the **mix** of spend toward primary/preventive activities (share of spend and staff time), with downstream effects on avoidable admissions. 
_H4:_ Effects are heterogeneous: stronger where pre‑reform hospital–ACES boundaries overlapped tightly and where risk‑stratification tooling is rapidly adopted. ([acss.min-saude.pt](https://www.acss.min-saude.pt/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Circular_Normativa_25_2024.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "N. 25/2024/ACSS DATA: 2024-10-10 CIRCULAR NORMATIVA PARA: ULS e IPO"))

**Research design, identification & data.** 
Use an _event‑study/DID_ design around the policy shock windows: 
i) **DL 102/2023** enactment (7 Nov 2023) listing which mergers occur; 
ii) **go‑live 1 Jan 2024** for nationwide ULS rollout; 
iii) **DL 54/2024** (6 Sep 2024) removing ARS as a potential confounder in regional budget flows. 
Treat **IPOs** as a contemporaneous comparison group and exploit **staggered intensity** using pre‑reform _alignment metrics_ (share of a hospital’s catchment already served by the paired ACES; number of ACES per hospital; pre‑reform referral concentration). 
Use _Sun–Abraham_ style estimators for staggered treatment and pre‑trend checks. 
Primary data sources: 
a) **Diário da República** texts of DL 102/2023 and DL 54/2024; 
b) **SNS** rollout note (1 Jan 2024); 
c) **ACSS Circulars**—No. 25/2024 on centralized payments; No. 4/2024 on risk‑stratification; No. 15/2023 on the **e‑Qualidade** platform; 
d) **Portal da Transparência SNS** for performance and activity; 
e) **ULS Relatório & Contas 2024** (several now public) for capex, digital investment, arrears, functional cost shares; 
f) **SPMS** joint billing adaptation note (for process timing). 
Outcome families: share of capex in digital/measurement; PROM/PREM coverage via e‑Qualidade; days‑payable‑outstanding and arrears; share of spend in primary care; ACS‑avoidable admissions; ED pressure; waiting times. ([files.diariodarepublica.pt](https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2023/11/21500/0000400020.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "PRESIDÊNCIA DO CONSELHO DE MINISTROS - Diário da República"))

**Methods.** 
Panel DID with event‑time coefficients; triple‑differences exploiting _payment centralization_ exposure (ULS vs. IPO; pre/post CN 25/2024); machine‑learning‑aided controls for case‑mix and local demand (risk‑stratification bands from ACSS); investment–cash‑flow sensitivity regressions (internal finance proxy) at provider‑year level; falsification tests using pre‑2019 ULS (e.g., Matosinhos) to validate design. ([acss.min-saude.pt](https://www.acss.min-saude.pt/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Circular-Normativa_4_Estratificacao-pelo-risco.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "N. 4/2024/ACSS DATA: 2024-02-15 CIRCULAR NORMATIVA PARA: Unidades ..."))

**Theoretical & managerial contribution.** 
To internal capital markets theory: show how **organizational integration** in a public health conglomerate changes the _allocation rule_ under soft budget constraints; 
To VBHC implementation: quantify how integrating **governance + payments + risk tools** shifts the _capital frontier_ toward value infrastructure. 
Managerially, provide **capital‑allocation playbooks** for ULS Boards: a rules‑based “VBHC investment gate” that ties discretionary capex envelopes to risk‑stratified population need and measured outcomes capacity, consistent with national circulars. ([Diário da República](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/102-2023-223906278?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Decreto-Lei n.º 102/2023, de 7 de novembro | DR - Diário da República"))

**Feasibility & risks.** 
Feasible because legal milestones, circulars and provider accounts are public; early ULS 2024 **Relatórios & Contas** are posted (e.g., Leiria; Matosinhos), enabling first‑pass coding. 
Main risks are common‑trend violations and concurrent shock contamination (e.g., ARS extinction); mitigate with IPO comparators, event‑time plots, and robustness to alternative controls and timing. ([ulsrl.min-saude.pt](https://www.ulsrl.min-saude.pt/informacao-publica/relatorios/relatorio-e-contas/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Relatório e Contas – ULS RL | Unidade Local de Saúde da Região de Leiria"))
